Character
public/valid/valid.glb is derived from the user-supplied standing base-2.obj.
Source SHA256: 8e805495c3851347a11de36df5be2efbb1df5920956e203bc15fcc3d1e0823b5
500,000 original triangles -> 29,994 triangles, 14,965 vertices, 14 bones.
GLB size: 1,349,264 bytes. Embedded clips: walk, idle, wave.
Original OBJ and walking MP4 were not modified or uploaded to the website.
The walking video is a visual reference, not extracted skeletal motion capture.
Colors use the approved standing artwork projected onto vertex colors plus
procedural rear colors. This is a web-optimized stylized rig, not a film-quality
facial rig or hand-painted 360-degree texture atlas.
Reproducible preparation scripts, source metrics and tests remain in the sibling
MooseTVs-Valid-lab folder. No paid animation service is required at runtime.

Implementation
src/redesign/valid.mjs: localized document stages, range illustration, controls.
src/browser/valid.js: one WebGL renderer, loaded on pages with a character stage;
14-bone animation, accessories, page-relative placement, bounded transitions.
src/browser/valid-core.mjs: pure route, curve and navigation helpers.
src/browser/motion.js: typography/chart/scenes and saved motion preferences.
public/valid.css: reserved in-content space, mobile and RTL styling.
Three.js is dynamically split out of the main authentication bundle.
No WebGL renderer, 3D module or model download on account and enquiry routes.
Animations rest after their sequence; offscreen/hidden tabs stop rendering.
The OS reduced-motion preference overrides the saved motion setting.
Hide Valid and Pause motion persist on this device. Welcome runs once per tab;
Replay welcome is optional. Skip, Escape and scroll dismiss it immediately.
Navigation retains regular modified-click/new-tab behavior and a 540ms maximum
character transition. Links work normally without JavaScript or WebGL.
All character labels and pricing text have English, Hebrew and Arabic copies.
